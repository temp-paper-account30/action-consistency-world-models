Action Consistency World Models

Anonymous authors. Under review.

Task

A four-fingered hand rotates an object to a goal orientation, observing one camera and its joint angles. Simulator rollouts of the expert policy.

Open-loop rollouts

Recorded actions replayed in both engines from the same 2 s context. The trace is the frozen policy's action difference on imagined versus real frames, normalized by the recorded actions' magnitude.

Closed-loop rollouts

The policy acts inside the simulator and inside the world model from the same 2 s context. Each clip is 120 steps at 12 Hz. The trace is the per-frame action divergence between the two engines, with the simulator's own re-run in gray. Labels give the true success rate of the model's training policy and of the evaluated policy.

Held-out policies

A model trained on a strong policy evaluates weak policies faithfully. A model trained on a weak policy breaks when a strong policy drives it.

Failure cases

The object leaves the hand and the model keeps it in place, or a strong policy inside a weak model dissolves the object.
